# ADR 0001 — Record architecture decisions

**Date:** 2026-07-23 · **Status:** accepted

## Decision

Every significant technical decision gets a short ADR in `docs/adr/`, numbered
sequentially. An ADR states the context, the decision, and the consequences —
a few paragraphs, not a specification. ADRs are never rewritten to say something
different; a superseding decision gets a new ADR that references the old one.

## Why

The charter (CLAUDE.md §6) requires this project to stay understandable across
many iterations and many agent/human sessions. The *why* behind decisions is the
first thing lost between sessions; ADRs are the cheapest durable fix.
